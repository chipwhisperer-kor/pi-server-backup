Privacy policy

pi-server-backup is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive with the "drive.file" permission, which limits it to the files it creates itself. It uploads the owner's own backup files and reads them back for checks and restores. It does not read other Drive files, does not share, sell or send any data to anyone, and keeps its login token only on the owner's own server.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
